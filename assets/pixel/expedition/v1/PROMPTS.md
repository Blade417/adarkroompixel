# 首次远行样片生成提示词

内置 image_gen.imagegen；beast-final 是对 beast-draft.png 的透明背景编辑，仅调整分帧布局。所有原始 PNG 保持不改写；运行时按 manifest 裁取与采样。

## background

Use case: stylized-concept. Production game asset, a clean empty forest battle background for Emberveil, a restrained dark pixel-art survival game. Wide 16:9 composition. True carefully clustered low-resolution pixel art, intended to downsample to 576x324. Dense cold grey pine forest receding in mist across upper half, broken dark trunks frame the far left and far right. Large quiet flat forest clearing in the lower half, earthy charcoal brown soil and a few sparse dull dead grass tufts. The left and right standing positions at x25% and x73%, y77% must remain empty and readable. Moody cold twilight, muted blue grey and weathered wood brown, narrow warm grey ground highlights. No snow on ground, no saturated green, no characters, no creatures, no wood gathering props, no buildings, no UI, no text, no logo, no borders. Do not bake shadows shaped like characters. Crisp pixel edges, no blur, no painterly brushstrokes.

## traveler

Use case: stylized-concept. Production pixel-art game sprite sheet on actual fully transparent background, one anonymous hooded traveler, worn charcoal grey cloak, leather boots, small brown rucksack, pale bone-tipped spear, restrained cold grey and weathered brown palette. No face detail, no glowing magic. Six isolated full-body sprites arranged in a regular grid 3 columns x 2 rows with very large empty spacing. Every sprite fully visible including feet and spear with no clipping. TOP ROW: left a side-view traveler facing RIGHT holding spear upright in idle stance; middle the SAME traveler facing RIGHT thrusting spear horizontally; right the SAME traveler facing RIGHT recoiling slightly from a hit. BOTTOM ROW: left a small three-quarter overhead world-map view facing NORTH, middle three-quarter overhead facing SOUTH, right three-quarter overhead facing EAST. Ground-level side-view top-row figures same body height, feet aligned on shared baseline within row. Bottom-row overhead map figures same body height and consistent clothing. Intended logical side-view frame 48x64, map figure16x24. Genuine clustered pixel art, crisp low-res edges, simple large readable shapes, no antialiasing, no words, no labels, no arrows, no background, no shadows spread outside feet, no visible grid. Keep every cell isolated on transparency.

## beast

Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent pixel-art sprite sheet of one snarling feral wolf-like four-legged beast from a bleak survival game, facing LEFT. Low hunched muscular body, ragged dark umber-grey fur, hunched shoulder, narrow muzzle, a few pale visible teeth, restrained amber eyes without glow. Three isolated poses of the SAME creature in a single horizontal row with large transparent gaps: left idle crouching and snarling; middle lunging forward with jaws open; right recoiling from a hit. All poses same body size and matching anatomy, entire tail, ears, paws visible with no clipping. Intended logical sprite size about72x52. Crisp intentionally chunky low-resolution pixel clusters, cold charcoal grey and desaturated brown, subtle pale fur accents so the shape reads against dark forest, no bright fantasy colors. Actual fully transparent RGBA background, no words, no labels, no grid lines, no scenery, no other characters, no gore, no painted floor or broad cast shadow.

## beast-final

Edit this production pixel-art wolf beast sprite sheet. Preserve the same beast identity, chunky pixel clusters, muted charcoal brown palette, LEFT-facing direction, and the three poses. Change ONLY the layout: make three evenly sized separate square cells in one horizontal row, with each creature scaled down to fit INSIDE the CENTRAL 70 percent of its cell. Leave wide fully transparent gutters between the entire bounding boxes of the three creatures. Nothing from any creature may extend into the neighboring cell. Keep ALL tails, ears, paws inside the image, with large transparent outer margins. Baselines aligned, same body height in all poses. Exact true transparent RGBA background, no painted floor, no broad shadow, no labels or cell borders. This sheet is going to be cut into three equal cells, so clear empty vertical gutters at one-third and two-thirds of total image width are essential.

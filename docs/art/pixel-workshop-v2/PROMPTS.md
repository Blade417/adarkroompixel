使用内置 `image_gen.imagegen`；每件物品单独生成，透明背景。输入参考依次为 v1 对应精灵、原版 room-back.png、v1 游戏内截图。生成原图保持不变，运行时裁取 alpha 可见区域并缩小。

## cart

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Image 1 is the existing handcart sprite to redesign; image 2 is the authoritative cabin art style, palette and camera reference; image 3 shows its previous placement and the visual mismatch to fix. Produce ONLY the redesigned isolated handcart sprite on real transparent alpha, not a cabin or a composite scene. Preserve its identity as a primitive handmade wooden firewood cart with a small load of logs, no motor. Reorient it into the cabin's slightly elevated, nearly frontal side-on camera, with a little of the cart-bed top visible, and a small perspective recession matching the floorboards in reference 2. The cart is parked against the left wall by the door, handles pointing left and a little back; show a single simple front wooden wheel on a single axle and the far wheel partly behind it, no decorative wagon spokes or bright metal hardware. Make a modest compact utilitarian object: proportions suited to approximately 74 pixels wide and 36 pixels tall in the 480x270 cabin. Use the SAME broad hard-edged flat pixel clusters, sparse wood-grain strokes and low-detail weathered plank treatment as the wall beams in image 2. Very restrained dark cool grey-brown wood, charcoal blue-grey shadows and only a few muted brown edge highlights on the right. Dark edges should merge with the cabin, not a heavy black sticker outline. No golden rim light, orange logs, shiny rivets, glow, gradients, miniature-model detailing, painted texture or antialiased edges. The sprite itself should be evenly dimly lit so the game can add its own hearth lighting. Keep all wheels and handles completely in frame with transparent margin, no ground plane, floor patch, baked cast shadow, text, UI or characters. Center the single prop, completely isolated, with genuine transparent background.
```
